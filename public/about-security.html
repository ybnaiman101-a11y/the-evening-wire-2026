About THE LOUNGE

THE LOUNGE is an independently operated community website for everyday conversations, pictures, videos, polls, and social posts. Browsing posts is public. Posting, reacting, following, and voting require a member account.

Accounts

Existing accounts from the former Evening Wire website continue to work. To create an account, you enter a display name, email address, recovery password, and your own four-digit sign-in PIN. Your email is not verified by this website. Use a unique password and PIN that you do not use elsewhere.

What other people can see

Posts, replies, images, videos, voice notes, your display name and profile picture are public to people visiting the website. Do not share private details in public conversations. If you follow someone, the public directory can show your follow relationship. No direct/private messages are provided.

The Friends directory is optional: every member starts hidden. You must deliberately opt in before other visitors can find your profile in that directory. Your email address is not shown there.

Privacy and security

The site owner can access member names, submitted email addresses, registration dates and last-login information through restricted administrator tools. Aggregate activity and traffic statistics may be recorded. Passwords and PINs are stored as one-way salted hashes; sign-in uses HTTP-only cookies. The public site is served over HTTPS using Railway hosting.

There is no guarantee of absolute privacy or security on any public social website. The owner can moderate or remove public posts. Current member tools let you change or remove your profile picture and sign out. Deleting an existing member account is not currently offered in the interface.

Recognize the correct site

Use the website's current public domain: https://electionhub2026.com.co/. The domain address has not yet changed with the site's social rebrand. Do not type your PIN or password on a different or suspicious URL.
