Already registered before PINs were introduced? Your old account is still there. Enter the same email and original password you registered with, and choose a new four-digit PIN. You do not need to create a second account or wait for an earlier incorrect-PIN lockout to expire.

For security, five incorrect PIN attempts trigger a 30-minute account lockout. The PIN is not emailed or texted; it is a code you choose. Email ownership is not yet verified.
